### The two years allocate the functional columns by different methods

On the 2024 return, ten indirect lines carry the **same three percentages** — occupancy, depreciation, insurance, interest, accounting, legal, office, dues, real estate taxes and meals are each split 80.5% / 15.7% / 3.9%. That is one overhead allocation ratio applied across the return.

The 2025 figures do not do that. Every line takes the 990 function recorded on the classification behind it, so a line is wholly one column or wholly another — except the compensation block, which is split by the effort distribution.
