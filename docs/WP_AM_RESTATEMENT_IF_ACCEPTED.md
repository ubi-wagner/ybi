# Workpaper — what happens if America Makes accepts the rate and lets YBI restate

Prepared 13 September 2026. Scenario, as instructed: **NCDMM accepts fringe
21.90% and combined indirect 43.99% on MTDC, and permits restatement of prior
invoices at any time.** Every figure computed against the sealed 2025 record.

---

## The headline, before the detail

**Restating 2025 across the three contracts is a net give-back of $10,575.11,
not a windfall.** One contract gains, two lose:

| | as billed 2025 | restated | movement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Last Tactical Mile** | 368,222.24 | 475,905.76 | **+107,683.52** |
| Drive AM | 579,240.87 | 520,454.56 | **−58,786.31** |
| Hybrid Phase 2 | 191,638.05 | 132,165.73 | **−59,472.32** |
| **total** | **1,139,101.16** | **1,128,526.05** | **−10,575.11** |

**The value in accepting the rate is not in restating the past.** It is in two
other places, and §5 and §6 are the ones to read:

- **$203,790.29 a year of indirect on non-labour that nothing recovers today.**
- **A ceiling conversation.** Inside the existing ceilings, accepting 43.99%
  costs YBI **$674,366.59 of scope**. That is the negotiation, not the
  restatement.

## 1. Why restating is a rebuild, not an addition

This is the trap and everything below depends on it.

YBI does not currently bill an indirect line. It recovers indirect **inside
the labour rate** — billing staff time at about **1.77×** what the person
costs, where full burden is 1.44×. So a restated invoice cannot keep the
loaded labour rate *and* add an indirect line. That would charge indirect
twice.

A correct restatement rebuilds the invoice:

1. **Labour comes down** to actual cost — wages plus fringe at 21.90%.
2. **Non-labour** is stated at actual cost.
3. **One indirect line** is added at 43.99% of the two together.

| | billed labour | labour at cost | labour comes **down** | indirect goes **on** |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Drive AM | 304,483.80 | 179,571.00 | −124,912.80 | +159,002.68 |
| Last Tactical Mile | 89,922.24 | 64,128.99 | −25,793.25 | +145,392.70 |
| Hybrid Phase 2 | 172,415.95 | 76,788.03 | −95,627.92 | +40,377.60 |
| **total** | **566,821.99** | **320,488.02** | **−246,333.97** | **+344,772.98** |

The indirect line is larger than the labour reduction — **+$98,439.01** — and
the rest of the movement is the non-labour, §3.

## 3. The non-labour has its own problem, and it blocks the restatement

Restating to cost only works if the cost record is complete. It may not be:

| | billed as other direct | cost classified to the contract | difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Drive AM | 274,757.07 | 181,880.88 | **+92,876.19** |
| Last Tactical Mile | 233,900.00 | 266,384.07 | −32,484.07 |
| Hybrid Phase 2 | 19,222.10 | 15,000.10 | +4,222.00 |
| **total** | **527,879.17** | **463,265.05** | **+64,614.12** |

**Drive AM billed $92,876.19 more in ODCs and materials than the ledger shows
as Drive AM cost.** Two possible explanations and they lead opposite ways:

- **The classification under-attributes.** Real Drive AM cost is sitting in an
  account that was judged to another objective. Then the restated claim is too
  low and the credit is overstated.
- **The billing over-claimed.** Then the credit is right and larger.

**Do not issue a credit memo until this is resolved.** It is $92,876.19 on the
one contract with the largest credit. The 34 ledger lines in
`Grant Expenses:Drive AM` are the place to start, against the ODC line on
twelve invoices.

## 5. Where accepting the rate actually pays: non-labour

The loaded labour rate recovers indirect on **labour only**. A negotiated
rate recovers it on **all modified total direct cost** — including the
consultants, subcontracts, ODCs and materials that currently carry nothing at
all.

| | non-labour 2025 | indirect it would carry | |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Last Tactical Mile | 266,384.07 | **117,182.35** | consultant-heavy — the big winner |
| Drive AM | 181,880.88 | 80,009.40 | mostly ODCs |
| Hybrid Phase 2 | 15,000.10 | 6,598.54 | |
| **total** | **463,265.05** | **203,790.29** | |

**$203,790.29 a year, on 2025 volumes, that the current method cannot reach.**
That is the recurring value of accepting the rate, and it is roughly twenty
times the one-off restatement.

It also explains the pattern in §2. LTM gains because it is 81% non-labour;
Drive AM and Hybrid lose because they are labour-heavy and the loaded rate was
already over-recovering on that labour.

## 6. The ceiling, which is the actual negotiation

**A ceiling caps direct and indirect together.** Today's budgets allocate
almost all of it to direct. Accept 43.99% inside an unchanged ceiling and the
direct budget has to shrink to make room:

| | ceiling | direct budget today | direct budget at 43.99% | indirect | **scope lost** |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Drive AM | 1,103,594 | 1,103,594.00 | 766,437.95 | 337,156.05 | **(337,156.05)** |
| Last Tactical Mile | 899,500 | 817,728.00 | 624,696.16 | 274,803.84 | **(193,031.84)** |
| Hybrid Phase 2 | 512,409 | 500,043.00 | 355,864.30 | 156,544.70 | **(144,178.70)** |
| **total** | **2,515,503** | **2,421,365.00** | **1,746,998.41** | **768,504.59** | **(674,366.59)** |

**Accepting the rate inside these ceilings costs YBI $674,366.59 of work
delivered.** Same money, less programme.

So the ask is not "please accept our rate." It is:

> **Accept the rate, and increase the ceilings by the indirect it makes
> explicit — or accept that scope reduces by the same amount.**

Either answer is legitimate and NCDMM chooses. What YBI must not do is accept
the rate and quietly deliver the original scope, which is the current position
in a different costume.

## 7. If the facilities carve-out lands, this all gets worse

The 43.99% carries **no 2 CFR 200.465 facilities carve-out** because no
building square footage is on the record. When Kelly's measurement arrives the
rate falls. At the provisional 20% tenant share it is 37.67%:

| | restated at 43.99% | restated at 37.67% | as billed | movement at 37.67% |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Drive AM | 520,454.56 | 497,610.80 | 579,240.87 | (81,630.07) |
| Last Tactical Mile | 475,905.76 | 455,017.33 | 368,222.24 | 86,795.09 |
| Hybrid Phase 2 | 132,165.73 | 126,364.72 | 191,638.05 | (65,273.33) |
| **total** | **1,128,526.05** | **1,078,992.85** | **1,139,101.16** | **(60,108.31)** |

**The give-back goes from $10,575.11 to $60,108.31.** LTM's claim falls from
$107,683.52 to $86,795.09.

**Do not restate before the square footage is measured.** Restating at 43.99%
and then discovering the rate is 37.67% means YBI over-claimed on a rate it
proposed itself, which is worse than not having restated.

## 8. If NCDMM accepts, the sequence

**Do not restate 2025 as a package.** Three separate moves, in this order.

**First — the things that must be true before anything is issued:**

1. **Resolve the $92,876.19** of Drive AM non-labour (§3). Until then the
   credit memo may be wrong in YBI's favour or against it.
2. **Get the square footage** and fix the rate (§7). Restating twice is worse
   than restating late.
3. **Certify the labour.** 0 of 43. Every restated invoice claims labour cost
   at 100% of the reconstruction, and 2 CFR 200.430(i) goes to the
   allowability of the whole of it. Restating *raises* the reliance on the
   reconstruction, because the labour line becomes the audited cost rather
   than a rate.

**Then — the paperwork, per contract:**

4. **A §4.4 modification each**, changing the basis from the 10% de minimis to
   the negotiated rate, and stating the effective date.
5. **A budget realignment each.** Drive AM's and Hybrid's Schedule B contain
   **no INDIRECT category at all** — the restated invoices bill a line their
   agreements do not have. LTM has one at $81,772.76 and the 2025 restatement
   alone needs $145,392.70 of it.
6. **The ceiling decision** (§6), settled in the same modification. This is
   the one with $674,366.59 on it.

**Then — the transactions:**

7. **LTM: issue the invoice for $107,683.52.** It stands on its own merits and
   does not depend on the other two. It is also the contract whose schedule
   already budgets indirect at exactly 10.00% of direct — the cleanest case in
   the file.
8. **Drive AM and Hybrid: issue the credit memos**, $58,786.31 and
   $59,472.32. Raise these yourself. An organisation that brings its own
   over-recovery to the table is one whose $107,683.52 claim gets read
   generously.

**Then — going forward:**

9. **Bill labour at cost plus an explicit indirect line** from the effective
   date. The 1.77× loaded rate stops.
10. **Recover indirect on non-labour** — §5, $203,790.29 a year at 2025
    volumes, and the actual reason to do any of this.

## 9. What could go wrong

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **NCDMM reads the credits as an admission** and audits the whole relationship. Mitigated by raising them first, with the workpaper, rather than being found. |
| **The $674,366.59 of scope is not funded** and YBI accepts the rate anyway. Then YBI delivers 28% less programme across the three contracts and the recovery gains nothing. |
| **The rate moves after restatement.** §7. |
| **The labour reconstruction does not survive certification.** Every restated labour line is that reconstruction at 100%. If people correct their sheets materially, the restated invoices move. |
| **Drive AM is closed.** Period of performance ended 4 January 2026. A credit is fine; an additional claim would not have been. |
| **A prior-year pattern.** This workpaper covers 2025 only. If 2024 was billed the same way — a 1.77× loaded rate against a de minimis election — the same exercise applies to it, and the awards began in 2023 and 2024. **Nobody has looked at 2024.** |

## 10. One sentence for the board

*Accepting a negotiated indirect rate is worth roughly $200,000 a year going
forward and costs about $10,000 in one-off adjustments to 2025 — but only if
the contract ceilings move with it; inside today's ceilings it would cost
$674,000 of delivered programme.*
