3 · Classification, evidence and certification

Classification. Every expense is grouped by account and vendor, largest first; the top 200 groups carry 94.7% of the dollars. For each, Tom records four things — which cost pool, which column of the Form 990, whether it is allowable federally, and if direct, which contract. The system proposes an answer where it has a real signal (about two-thirds of the dollars) and proposes nothing where it does not, rather than guessing. Anything unjudged stays out of the calculation, so the rate reads high while work is unfinished — the honest direction to be wrong.

Evidence. Each judgment carries a stated quality of support, from verified against a document on file down to management reconstruction. A judgment claiming the highest grade is refused unless a document is actually attached. The audit package prints the grade beside every figure, so a reviewer sees what is proven and what is asserted without asking.

Certification. Where a salary is charged across several contracts, 2 CFR 200.430(i) requires that person to certify their own effort. Every employee therefore gets a timesheet and a certification screen, managers get a list of who has not yet signed, and the system will not let anyone sign on somebody else's behalf. YBI's 2025 labour distribution is currently a careful reconstruction from calendars and project records — which is defensible, and is not a certification. Turning it into one needs the roster above; until then the audit package says so on its first page, above the figures.

5 · America Makes — applying the rate to 2025

YBI billed America Makes by dividing the approved budget by twelve. That method cannot see what was actually spent, and it produced errors in both directions — some awards over-billed, some substantially under-billed.

The central fact is an absence. The approved budget for Drive AM funds seven cost categories against $583,594 of labour and contains no indirect line at all. The invoice YBI issued carries exactly the five categories the budget funded — three of them at zero — and no indirect line, because there was none to carry. That document is itself the record of what YBI was never budgeted to claim, and it is the strongest evidence the restatement has.

America Makes has indicated it will accept restated rates applied to prior invoices, provided the restated total stays within the contracted ceiling. That turns this from an argument into arithmetic: per award, restate at the defensible rate, cap at the ceiling, and the sign of the difference decides between an amended invoice and a credit. Hybrid, over-billed, produces a credit; the others produce claims. The system does this invoice by invoice and records the arithmetic beside each. A restatement carries the seal of the rate it used, stays a proposal until the sponsor agrees in writing, and cannot be finalised without naming the clause that authorised changing the basis.

Two things still block the numbers. The ceilings for Drive AM, Digital Engineering and Last Tactical Mile are not on file — only Hybrid's $500,043 is known, and the ceiling caps each claim. And two different "amounts billed" exist in YBI's own records, $1.58m apart, depending on whether you read the grant tabs or recognised revenue. Both have to be settled before anything is sent.

6 · Audit close-out and the Form 990

Both come out of the same sealed record, and neither is produced by hand. The audit package is a workbook of numbered schedules — import, reconciliation, classifications, rate build-up, awards — each figure traceable to the ledger line and the document behind it, each stating what is unfinished on its first sheet, because a workbook travels and the caveat must travel with it. Form 990 Part IX is assembled from the same classifications: every expense lands in programme services, management and general, or fundraising because somebody judged it so and said why.

The return prints NOT FILEABLE while any expense is unjudged, and cost nobody has classified appears in its own column rather than being spread across the three the return prints — the totals are deliberately short by that amount until the queue is empty. Nobody can file an incomplete return from this system by accident.

The order is fixed and each step is refused until the one before it is complete: classify → seal → compute the rate → allocate to contracts → restate the America Makes invoices → produce the package and the return.
