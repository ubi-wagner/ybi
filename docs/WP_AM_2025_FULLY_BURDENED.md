## 1. Why this is not the restatement

`POST /api/restate` rebuilds an invoice from the **cost record** — the labour
distribution's wages, the ledger's DIRECT classifications — and asks what the
year supports. That is the right question for a claim and the wrong question
for *what should this invoice have been*, because the cost record
under-attributes these awards.

| Drive AM, non-labour | |
| --- | ---: |
| ODC + materials + travel **on the invoices** | 274,757.07 |
| what the ledger classifies to Drive AM | 181,880.88 |
| **gap** | **92,876.19** |

Every one of those dollars was billed carrying **zero indirect**. Rebuilding
from the ledger throws the $92,876.19 away *and* the burden on it —
**$22,958.99** of overhead and G&A at 24.72%, so **$115,835.18** in all, on
one award. (At the recorded `INDIRECT_COMBINED` of 24.71% it is $115,825.90;
the build-up applies the parts, per §2.)

So this takes the cost elements **on the face of the invoices YBI issued** and
runs the structure up them. It is the answer to what this engagement has said
from the beginning: *no America Makes award budgets meaningful indirect, and
that is the recovery the restatement exists to go after.* The recurring value
is indirect on non-labour, which a loaded labour rate cannot reach at all.

## 2. The structure

| | certified | anticipated |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Fringe, of direct labour | **21.90%** | 21.90% |
| Overhead, of MTDC | **12.35%** | 8.13% |
| G&A, of MTDC | **12.37%** | 12.37% |
| combined (overhead + G&A) | **24.72%** | 20.50% |

**The combined rate reads two ways and both are on the record.** 12.35 + 12.37
is 24.72; `INDIRECT_COMBINED` is recorded at **24.71%**. The engine computes
each pool over the same base and rounds, so the sum of the parts and the
recorded combined differ in the last place. A build-up applies the parts,
because that is what a build-up is. It is stated rather than reconciled away —
a figure that appears twice at two values is the defect this engagement has
paid for more than any other, and one basis point on Drive AM's MTDC is
$64.59.

**What MTDC takes, per 2 CFR 200.1.** Labour, fringe, travel, materials,
consultant and other direct costs in full; the first $25,000 of each subaward;
equipment, fee and any indirect line out. `CONSULTANT` is in full because
200.331 makes these contractors rather than subrecipients — they deliver into
YBI's own programme against YBI's scope. `OTHER` is counted and **flagged**: a
category that says nothing about what it holds cannot be tested against an
exclusion.

## 3. Two readings of the labour line, and neither is asserted

The one genuine fork, worth six figures, so it is printed rather than decided.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **AS INVOICED** | the labour on the face is direct cost and carries full burden. Drive AM bills $304,483.80. |
| **LABOUR AT COST** | the labour on the face is a loaded rate that already holds indirect, so it comes down to the reconstruction's wages plus fringe before anything goes on top. Drive AM is $179,571.00. |

The ratio between them is **1.70× on Drive AM and 2.25× on Hybrid**, against a
full burden of about 1.5×, which reads as a loaded rate. But the labour
distribution is a **management reconstruction**, and if it under-attributes
labour the way it demonstrably under-attributes non-labour, that ratio is an
artefact of incomplete attribution rather than evidence of loading.

**Settling this is the single highest-value question in the file.** It is
worth $462,047.66 between the two columns below.
