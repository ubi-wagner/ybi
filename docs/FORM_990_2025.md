### How the compensation is split

The pool judgment on the wage and fringe accounts is `EXCLUDED`, and that is
right about the **rate**: `compute` already feeds the labour distribution into
MTDC through `add_labor()`, so putting the wage accounts in a pool as well
would count the payroll twice. It says nothing about which column of the
**return** a salary belongs in.

So the compensation split comes from the effort distribution — the same
source the fringe base comes from, and for the same reason the eleventh
control insists on it:

| function | wages distributed | share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Program | $1,530,513.48 | 83.4046% |
| Management and general | $264,444.89 | 14.4108% |
| Fundraising | $40,088.80 | 2.1846% |

Read through `cost_objective.objective_type`, which already carries what each
objective is for. Largest remainder against the category total, so the three
add back to the ledger to the cent.

## What the return rests on that is not finished

* **0 of 363 federally chargeable judgments cite a document.** Attaching is
  not citing: a citation is why a judgment was made, and it is what
  `VERIFIED` requires. No figure on Part IX moves when they land.
* **No 200.430(i) certification has been signed.** The whole $1,835,047.17
  labour distribution is management reconstruction. It does not change the
  functional split — the distribution is the same document either way — but
  it is what an auditor will ask for first.
* **The square footage and the asset funding are estimates.** They do not
  reach Part IX at all: the carve-outs are a rate adjustment and the return
  reports cost as the ledger holds it. They are named here because the same
  set carries both papers and a reader should not have to work out which
  figures they touch.

## Two figures a reader will try to reconcile and should not

* **Part IX totals $6,775,212.18 and the classification scope is
  $10,180,642.10.** The return's scope is the P&L's Expense and COGS sections;
  the classification's is those plus Other Income, which 2 CFR 200.406 makes
  a reduction of cost rather than revenue. They are different questions and
  both are right.
* **The functional total is not the rate's pools.** $6.78m across three
  functions and $1.17m of indirect pool over a $4.74m base are two different
  cuts of the same ledger. The rate excludes what the pools must not carry —
  the wage accounts, the applicable credits and the 200.436(b) depreciation —
  and the return does not.
