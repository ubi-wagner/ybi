## Defects found and fixed

**1. The audit package hid the difference between "disagrees" and "no data".**
`v_statement_reconciliation` and `v_asset_control` both compute `state`, and
the Controls sheet emitted only the variance and a boolean. With no asset
register on file, ASSET_REGISTER printed `-850382.89` and `Ties=False` — which
a reviewer reads as *the register disagrees with the ledger by the whole of
its depreciation*, when the truth is *there is no register*. That is the exact
misreading migration 029 was written to prevent, surviving into the one
artefact that leaves the building. Both sheets now carry `State`, and the row
reads `NO DATA`.

**2. `drive_year` never learned the eleventh control.** The payroll register
was added after the drive was written. The drive sealed, asked for a rate,
received a correct 409 from the reconciliation gate, and reported the gate
working as a failure. It now does what the controller does: finds the donor
credit sitting in the intern wage account, names it, and writes down the
residual.

**3. A refused request was reported as an unaudited change.** The `mutating`
guard fires when the audit count does not rise, and said "changed the database
and wrote nothing to the audit log". A refused request changes nothing and
writes nothing, correctly — so every 409 produced a second, false finding, and
it was the one a reader chases first. The guard now stands down when the
wrapped call already reported why it did not proceed.

**4. The reconciliation page said ten points.** It has said eleven since the
payroll register was added; the header stat was right and the prose was not.
Fixed, along with the same claim in CLAUDE.md.

**5. The manual photographed drive fixtures.** Running the walk after the
drives put three documents called "Access drive" onto the landing page in the
manual. `prove.sh` orders the walk before the drives for exactly this reason
and an ad-hoc run broke it. Re-shot against an instance carrying real data and
nothing else. **Worth hardening**: the walk should build its own instance
rather than depend on the order somebody runs things in.

## One finding left open, deliberately

**The payroll control's register side moves while the year is being
certified.** `v_labor_effective` prefers a submitted timesheet over the
reconstruction it replaces — correctly, that is the point — so every
certification that lands moves `register_wages`. The two round differently at
the cent. In the first full-year run the drive named a 53.25 residual when the
register read `...19`, two timesheets landed, the register settled at `...18`,
and the control re-opened at **−0.01**. `POST /api/rates/compute` then refused
to produce a rate over one cent.

The gate is right and the arithmetic is right. What is wrong is the order:
a residual named in March can be a cent out in December through nobody's
fault.

**This was not fixed with a tolerance, and should not be.** A tolerance that
swallows a cent is the same machinery that would swallow a thousand, which is
what the fences on `ROUNDING` exist to prevent. The drive now reconciles the
register immediately before sealing — which is the order the engagement runs
in anyway: reconcile, seal, then rate.

What a real controller should be told, and currently is not: *this control
re-opened because the base moved, not because anything is wrong.* That is a
message the Reconcile screen could carry and does not. Recommended, not built.

## Two things the drive proves and does not prove

`drive_year` computes a rate of **37.71%** on a G&A pool of 1,678,057.27 over
an MTDC base of 4,450,232.39, allocates it, and restates three invoices
against it, with both proofs tying to the cent.

**That is not YBI's indirect rate and must not be quoted as one.** It is the
mechanism working over six classified groups — the drive's own fixtures. The
FRINGE pool in that run is empty and its rate computes to 0.0000, which ties
trivially. What the drive establishes is that seal → rate → allocation →
restatement holds end to end, that the seal travels with the rate, and that
neither proof can be skipped. What it does not establish is any number.

The real rate needs the 999 classifications and 43 certifications that are
still open — see `docs/BASELINE_2025.md`.

## Reproducing this

```bash
# a clean database, the real exports, no fixtures
scripts/provision.py --base $BASE
scripts/load_2025.py  --base $BASE      # GL, P&L, balance sheet
scripts/load_labor.py                    # the effort distribution
scripts/load_invoices.py                 # the three America Makes invoices
scripts/reconcile.py  --base $BASE --record
scripts/walk_manuals.py --base $BASE     # before any drive
scripts/drive_everyone.py --base $BASE
scripts/drive_access.py   --base $BASE
scripts/drive_actors.py   --base $BASE

# drive_year needs a database of its own — it walks a year forward and
# nothing in it can happen twice
scripts/drive_year.py --base $OTHER_BASE
```

`prove.sh` runs all but `drive_year`, which needs its own database and is
therefore the one a reviewer has to start deliberately.
