## 1. What this asks for

YBI has closed 2025, classified every dollar of cost, sealed the
classifications before computing any rate, and developed an indirect cost rate
structure from the sealed set. Applying that structure to the four NCDMM
awards YBI billed in 2025 produces **four corrections, running in two
directions**:

| award | | |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Last Tactical Mile | YBI under-recovered and asks NCDMM for | **43,960.60** |
| Drive AM | YBI over-collected and returns to NCDMM | **128,474.23** |
| Hybrid Phase 2 | YBI over-collected and returns to NCDMM | **72,947.07** |
| Digital Engineering | YBI over-collected and returns to NCDMM | **320,427.12** |

**These are not added together in the cost record, and this memorandum states
them separately first for that reason.** Money YBI is owed and money YBI owes
are two different conversations, and a single net figure hides both. The
aggregate in §6 is a *settlement mechanism* offered for convenience, not a
restatement of what the record says.

YBI proposes to settle all four for 2025 in full and finally, by a single
credit in whichever direction the parties agree the aggregate should be struck.

## 2. The regulations relied on

Every figure below rests on 2 CFR Part 200. The provisions actually used, and
what each one decided:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **200.414(f)** | The de minimis election. All four awards elect it. **This is the provision the restatement turns on**: 200.414(f) charges a flat percentage of modified total direct cost, and it does not contemplate indirect recovered inside an undisclosed loaded labour rate, which is how three of these four were actually billed. |
| **200.414(c)** | A negotiated rate, once established, is the rate. YBI has now developed one; this memorandum is the request to move the four awards onto it under §4.4 of each agreement. |
| **200.1** | The definition of modified total direct cost — direct salaries and wages, applicable fringe, materials, supplies, services, travel, and subawards up to the first $25,000 each. Equipment, capital expenditure, rental of facilities, participant support and the portion of each subaward over $25,000 are excluded. This is the base every indirect rate below is taken over. |
| **200.331** | Subrecipient or contractor. YBI's portfolio consultants deliver into YBI's own incubation programme against YBI's scope, so they are **contractors** and count in the base in full rather than to the first $25,000. |
| **200.430(i)** | Compensation — personal services, and the records required to support it. See §7: no certification has yet been signed for 2025. |
| **200.431(b)** | Paid leave as a fringe benefit cost. YBI pays leave through the wage accounts as regular compensation — the leave-charged-as-salary method — so leave is inside the fringe **denominator** and nothing in the fringe pool is leave. Mixing the two methods would count leave twice. |
| **200.436(b)** | Depreciation on assets acquired with federal funds is unallowable. $156,235.27 of 2025 depreciation is carved out of the overhead pool on this provision. |
| **200.465** | Rental cost of buildings and equipment, and the treatment of space YBI owns and lets to third parties or holds vacant. $756,869.33 is carved out of the overhead pool on this provision, across five buildings. |
| **200.406** | Applicable credits. Refunds, rebates and adjustments reduce cost rather than being revenue. A $105,865.41 Employee Retention Tax Credit relating to **2020** is excluded from the 2025 pools on 200.406(b) rather than being taken as a 2025 credit. |
| **200.403** | Allowability generally — the test every classification was made against. |
| **200.405** | Allocability. Every indirect dollar is allocated to every benefiting objective, including fundraising and unallowable activity, which bear indirect and recover nothing. |
| **200.413** | Direct costs, and 200.413(c) on directly charging administrative functions. YBI has **not** direct-charged the controller's retainer to the three awards his hours log shows effort on, because 200.413(c) requires the charge to be in the budget or to have prior written approval and these awards have neither. |
| **200.501** | Single audit. Named because the answer for 2025 is open; see §7. |
| **Appendix IV to Part 200** | The indirect cost structure for non-profits, and specifically B.2–B.3 on the general administration pool. This is the authority for the treatment in §3 that puts YBI's own general and administrative labour **into** the G&A pool rather than treating it as a cost objective that receives an allocation. |

## 3. The developed indirect rate structure

Four rates, over two bases, from one sealed classification of the whole 2025
general ledger. Nothing was tuned and no rate was computed until the
classifications were sealed.

| | pool | base | rate |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| **Fringe** | 401,783.60 | 1,835,047.17 salaries and wages | **21.90%** |
| Overhead | 584,774.52 | 4,736,602.11 MTDC | 12.35% |
| General & administrative | 585,875.91 | 4,736,602.11 MTDC | 12.37% |
| **Indirect, combined** | **1,170,650.43** | **4,736,602.11 MTDC** | **24.71%** |

**The fringe rate is anchored at both ends and is not an estimate.** Its
numerator is the six accounts the profit and loss names as fringe — benefits,
FICA, 401k match, SUI, workers' compensation and FUTA — and its denominator is
the payroll register. 401,783.60 ÷ 1,835,047.17 = 0.2190. Neither figure was
chosen; the rate falls out.

**The combined rate is stated after $913,104.60 of carve-outs**, which is 61%
of the gross overhead pool of $1,497,879.12. Those carve-outs are the whole
reason the rate is 24.71% rather than 43.99%:

| | citation | amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Taft Technology Center — space let or vacant | 200.465 | 281,002.12 |
| Depreciation on federally funded assets | 200.436(b) | 156,235.27 |
| Tech Block Building 5 — space let or vacant | 200.465 | 149,498.82 |
| Semple Building — space let or vacant | 200.465 | 130,300.50 |
| America Makes Building — space let or vacant | 200.465 | 128,010.25 |
| YBI Main (Vindicator Building) — space let or vacant | 200.465 | 68,057.64 |
| | | **913,104.60** |

**Administrative labour is in the G&A pool, not a cost objective.** YBI's
general and administrative staff carry $264,444.90 of wages. Treating that as
a cost objective would allocate the indirect pool to its own administration,
which recovers from nobody. Appendix IV B puts the director's office,
accounting and personnel administration **in** the pool. This choice is
recorded on the rate itself as `admin_labour_basis = POOL`, and the
alternative treatment is on the record beside it: it would produce **43.99%**
rather than 24.71%. **YBI has adopted the treatment that produces the lower
rate.**

### Two things about this rate that work against YBI, stated here rather than found later

- **The building areas are derived, not measured.** Each building's area comes
  from the JobsOhio grant agreement's $116.27 per square foot of fixed asset
  investment applied to the 2024 audited statements; the let area comes from
  the rent roll at $7.00 per square foot. Every choice in that derivation took
  the low side, so the 200.465 carve-out is if anything **too small** and the
  rate correspondingly **too high**. A measured floor plan reached YBI on
  15 September 2026 and every building it can be compared on points the same
  way — see §7.
- **No vacancy allowance is claimed** beyond the space the rent roll shows as
  unlet, because nothing on the record measures any. Any vacancy actually
  measured makes the carve-out larger and this rate lower.

## 4. The offset on each award, and why each runs the way it does

Restating is a **rebuild, not an addition.** Three of these four awards billed
labour at a loaded rate with no indirect line at all, so the indirect recovery
was already inside the labour. A restated invoice therefore brings labour
**down** to wages plus fringe before the indirect line goes on. Adding an
indirect line to labour as billed would claim indirect twice.

Full burden on the certified rate is 1.2190 × 1.2471 = **1.52×** wages.

### Drive AM · SRA, prime FA8650-20-2-5700 · ceiling $1,103,594

Twelve invoices, $579,240.87, **no indirect line on any of them**. Labour was
billed at $304,483.80 against $179,571.00 of wages and fringe — **1.70×**,
against a full burden of 1.52×. Restated cost for the year is $450,766.64.

> **YBI returns $128,474.23.**

One matter is unresolved and YBI raises it rather than leaving it to be found:
Drive AM was billed $274,757.07 of non-labour — $272,493.96 of other direct
costs, $1,880.90 of materials and $382.21 of travel — against $181,880.88 that
the cost record classifies as Drive AM direct non-labour. That is a gap of
**$92,876.19**. Either the classification under-attributes cost to this award, in
which case the amount returned is overstated, or the original billing
over-claimed, in which case it is understated. YBI will reconcile this against
NCDMM's own records before the credit is struck.

### Hybrid Phase 2 · prime AFRL FA8650-20-2-5700 · ceiling $512,409 (Mod 001)

Nine invoices, $187,416.05, **no indirect line**. Labour billed at $172,415.95
against $76,788.03 of wages and fringe — **2.25×**, the heaviest loading of the
four. Restated cost is $114,468.98.

> **YBI returns $72,947.07.**

Hybrid was worked for a further quarter after the invoice register stops. The
general ledger carries $4,222.00 of further Hybrid billing in October, November
and December that the invoice register does not hold, against $18,137.41 of
cost in those months. The settlement figure above uses the **invoice register**,
because a settlement has to be against invoices NCDMM holds. The $4,222.00 is
named rather than netted and is a matter for the reconciliation.

### Last Tactical Mile · prime FA8650-20-2-5700 · ceiling $899,500

Twelve invoices, $368,222.24. **The only one of the four that ever billed an
indirect line** — $44,400.00, eleven months at $3,000 and one at $11,400 — and
the only one whose Schedule B budgets indirect, at 10.0000% of total direct.
It is also 63.5% consultant cost, $233,900.00, which makes its MTDC large
relative to its labour. Labour billed at 1.40×, below full burden. Restated
cost is $412,182.84.

> **YBI claims $43,960.60.**

This is the only award that runs in YBI's favour, and it does so *because* it
is the only one that attempted to claim indirect at all.

### Digital Engineering · SRA-0350, prime N00174-20-1-0031 · ceiling $1,000,690

Seven invoices, $579,074.25, period of performance ended 9 July 2025. **These
are not cost-reimbursement invoices in substance.** Each is an identical
$82,724.89, the last marked "(Final)", with no labour, materials, travel or
indirect line anywhere on the face — a fixed monthly draw. Restated cost for
the same seven months is $258,647.13.

> **YBI returns $320,427.12.** This is the largest single correction in the
> settlement and YBI raises it unprompted.

**This award's prime is not America Makes.** It flows from N00174-20-1-0031
through Energetics Technology Center and NSWC Indian Head, whereas the other
three flow from the AFRL America Makes cooperative agreement
FA8650-20-2-5700. NCDMM administers both. YBI's own invoices for this award
carry an "NCDMM — America Makes" bill-to on their face, which appears to be a
templating error rather than a statement about the flow-down. **Federal award
funds are not fungible between programmes**, so YBI's position is that this
$320,427.12 should be settled against the Navy/ONR flow-down separately and
should not be offset against the America Makes awards. §6 prices it both ways.

| | |

## 7. What is open, stated before signature rather than after

The rate certificate itself records the matters open at the moment it was
signed. YBI states them here because a figure on a letterhead is otherwise read
as settled.

1. **A measured floor plan arrived on 15 September 2026 and is not in this
   rate.** It is on file as evidence `EV-224fc92fe21b`. Where it can be
   compared with the derived estate it points one way on every building:

   | building | derived let/vacant | measured |
   | --- | ---: | ---: |
   | Taft Technology Center | 99.1% | ~100% |
   | America Makes Building | 99.9% | 100% |
   | Semple Building | 55.9% | ~100% |
   | Tech Block Building 5 | 33.2% | ~92% of assignable |
   | YBI Main (Vindicator) | 16.9% | ~83% of assignable |

   A larger let-and-vacant share means a **larger** 200.465 carve-out, a
   **smaller** federal overhead pool and a rate **below** 24.71%. A lower rate
   supports less indirect, so accepting the measurement would make each of the
   three credits to NCDMM **larger** and Last Tactical Mile's claim **smaller**.
   **The correction under this settlement therefore moves in NCDMM's favour,
   not YBI's.** Three rows of the reply cannot yet be read and are with YBI's
   facilities lead; the rate will be recomputed when they are answered.

2. **No 2025 effort certification has been signed** under 200.430(i). The whole
   $1,835,047.17 labour distribution is a management reconstruction from the
   payroll register and the hours log, rebuilt by the controller. It has not
   yet been read and signed by the forty-three people whose effort it was. This
   does not change any figure in this memorandum; it changes whether the direct
   labour charge is supportable, and YBI is putting the reconstruction in front
   of each person for signature.

3. **363 federally chargeable judgments do not yet cite a document.** Every one
   is classified and sealed; what is outstanding is the citation to the paper
   behind it.

4. **No payment is recorded against any invoice in YBI's register.** Whether
   each correction is an adjustment to a settled amount or to an outstanding
   one will be established in the reconciliation.

5. **The Single Audit question for 2025 is open.** The 2024 return answered
   "No" to whether an audit under Subpart F was required. 200.501 turns on
   federal expenditure in the year, and YBI holds four NCDMM awards and an EDA
   award. YBI is not carrying the 2024 answer forward.

## 8. What YBI is not asking for

- **No indirect is claimed on any award beyond what the restatement computes.**
  In particular YBI makes no claim for the two awards whose Schedule B contains
  no INDIRECT category at all — Drive AM, against $583,594 of budgeted labour,
  and Hybrid Phase 2, against $449,043. Those absences are evidence of what was
  budgeted; they are not treated here as an entitlement.
- **No prior year is reopened.** These awards began in 2023 and 2024 and this
  settlement covers 2025 only. If earlier years were billed on the same loaded
  labour rate, the same exercise would apply to them, and YBI is not proposing
  it.
- **No margin, fee or profit is included.** All four instruments are cost
  reimbursement, no fee.
- **The controller's retainer is not direct-charged.** His hours log places
  35.47% of his effort on three of these awards, worth $25,899.83 of cost
  sitting wholly in the G&A pool. Moving it to the awards would **lower** the
  combined rate to roughly 24.04% — about 0.67 points, on the recorded pools.
  YBI has not done so, because 200.413(c)
  requires the charge to be in the budget or to have prior written approval and
  these awards have neither.
