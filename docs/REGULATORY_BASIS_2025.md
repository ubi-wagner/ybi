# The rules the 2025 rate is built on

Youngstown Business Incubator · period 2025 · every figure read from the record

## Which body of rules applies — and which does not

All four America Makes awards are **subawards under federal financial
assistance**, not procurement contracts: three flow from AFRL cooperative
agreement **FA8650-20-2-5700**, and Digital Engineering (SRA-0350) from Navy
grant **N00174-20-1-0031** through Energetics Technology Center. All four are
cost reimbursement, no fee.

So **2 CFR 200 Subpart E governs allowability and the indirect rate, and FAR
Part 31 does not apply of its own force.** Two agreements say so on their face
— *"reimbursement only for costs allowable under 2 CFR 200 Subpart E"* (Drive
AM §4.2, LTM §4.2) — and Digital Engineering's Attachment 2 flow-down adds
**2 CFR 1103**, DoD's interim implementation of the Uniform Guidance.

**The FAR appears exactly once in the four executed agreements, and it is not
a cost principle:** 48 CFR 27.401, the definition of *data*, cited in the Data
Rights clause (§1.10) of Hybrid Phase 2 and Last Tactical Mile. No FAR 31, no
CAS, no 52.2xx cost clause reaches YBI on any of them.

## Four questions no amount of accounting settles

**200.331** on six parties — substance of the relationship, not what an invoice
called it. **200.430(i)** — nobody has signed, and the whole direct labour
charge rests on it. **200.465** — whether a client company in an incubator is a
tenant or programme space is a question about the tenancy agreements.
**200.406(b)** — which 2020 awards bore the wages behind the ERTC is not on
this record.
