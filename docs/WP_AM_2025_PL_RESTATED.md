## 3. The finding

**YBI has already recovered 100.9% of its fully burdened cost on these three
contracts in 2025 — and recorded 4.06% of it as indirect.** The recovery is
inside the labour rate:

| | billed labour | wages + fringe | multiple |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Drive AM | 304,483.80 | 179,571.00 | **1.70×** |
| Last Tactical Mile | 89,922.24 | 64,128.99 | **1.40×** |
| Hybrid Energy | 172,415.95 | 76,788.03 | **2.25×** |
| **total** | **566,821.99** | **320,488.02** | **1.77×** |

YBI bills labour at 1.77 times the loaded payroll cost the record distributes
to these contracts. The full burden computed from the sealed classification is
**1.44×**. So the labour rate is carrying the indirect — and carrying more of
it than the rate supports.

**This is a disclosure and compliance question, not a recovery opportunity.**
2 CFR 200.414(f) means the de minimis is charged as a rate on MTDC. It does
not contemplate indirect embedded in an undisclosed loaded labour rate on an
award that elected it. All four awards are set to `DE_MINIMIS_10`, and
`constraint_result` RATE_METHOD fails on every one.

**And restating on top would claim indirect twice.** $1,576,260.20 against
$1,128,526.05 of fully burdened cost is a 140% recovery, and it would consume
62.7% of all three ceilings in one year on multi-year awards.

### The two positions, which are never netted

| | |
| --- | ---: |
| **Last Tactical Mile, under-recovered** | **107,683.52** |
| **Drive AM and Hybrid, over-recovered** | **118,258.63** |

A $10,575.11 net is the least informative figure on this page. One is money to
ask for; the other is money that may be owed back. `v_restatement` is built so
the two can never be presented as one number, and the same rule applies here.

**LTM is the one with a real claim**, and it is the only contract of the three
whose Schedule B budgets indirect at all — $81,772.76, exactly 10.00% of its
direct budget. It billed $44,400.00 of that in 2025 and is $107,683.52 short
of its fully burdened cost.

finished taking *out* of the classification scope — correctly, because grant
income is not cost to classify, and incorrectly as a place to stop looking.

## 6. What follows

1. **Do not issue a restatement on these three contracts** on the strength of
   the rate alone. The year already recovers fully burdened cost.
2. **Get the loaded labour rate disclosed.** Find the rate sheet or basis of
   estimate behind the 1.77×, and establish what NCDMM was told. That is the
   document this workpaper is missing and it decides everything else.
3. **LTM is the claim.** $107,683.52 under-recovered, on the one contract
   whose schedule budgets indirect. It stands on its own and does not depend
   on the other two.
4. **Drive AM and Hybrid are the exposure.** $118,258.63 recovered above
   fully burdened cost, on awards whose schedules budget no indirect at all.
   Raise it before somebody else does.
5. **Settle the rate method.** RATE_METHOD fails on all four awards.
   Whichever way the disclosure question falls, the awards say de minimis and
   the billing does not behave like it.
6. **Certify.** 0 of 43, and every figure here rests on the reconstruction.

Two caveats carried from the rate itself: **no 2 CFR 200.465 facilities
carve-out is in the 43.99%** (no building is on the record), and $850,382.89
of depreciation sits in the pool at `PENDING` under 200.436(b). A carve-out
lowers the rate, which *widens* the over-recovery on Drive AM and Hybrid and
narrows LTM's claim. See `RATE_RECOMMENDATION.md` §4 for the band.
