- Every square foot accounted for — Needs the square footage per building, and what each part is used for. Until it lands the 200.465 carve-out cannot be computed at all, and every dollar of tenant and vacant occupancy cost sits in the federal pool.
- Every asset's funding source — 0.0% of the asset cost carries a funding source, so 200.436(b) cannot be answered on the depreciation the rest of it holds. It is the one column the schedule does not carry and 200.313(d)(1) requires.
- The paper behind the judgments — 0 of 363 federally chargeable judgments cite a document, of 757 live. Attaching is not citing: a citation is why the judgment was made, and it is what VERIFIED requires.
- The rate certified — A rate stands and nobody has put their name to it. Nothing is blocked by this — an invoice or a workbook produced now simply carries NOT CERTIFIED.
