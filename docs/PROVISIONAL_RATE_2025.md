## 1. What is an estimate, and which way it errs

Two of the largest adjustments in the model rest on figures nobody has
measured. Both are recorded as estimates, graded `TEST_ASSUMPTION` or
`MANAGEMENT_RECONSTRUCTION`, with their derivation in the `note` of every
row they produced — and both are taken at **the end of the range the
documents support that puts the least cost in the federal pool.**

That is deliberate and it runs against YBI. A rate that reads low recovers
less; a rate that reads high and is later cut is a repayment. Erring low is
the direction a reviewer can accept without argument.

### 1.1 The 200.465 facilities carve-out — $756,869.33

No building on the record carried square footage, so this could not fire at
all and every dollar of tenant and vacant occupancy cost was in the federal
pool. The estate is derived, not measured:

| **180,538 usable square feet** | the 2024 audited statements' Note 1 land, building and improvements of $21,098,684, less $107,530 of land, at that rate |
| **$7.00 a square foot** | the rate at which the 2025 rent roll accounts for 50.5% of that estate — the floor of the same note's *"predominately available to businesses in Mahoning Valley as operating leases"* |

| building | usable sq ft | let | let share | 2025 rent |

The rent roll ties to the ledger: `4021 TTC Rent` is Steelite's two Taft
leases to the cent, and `4026 NAMII Rent Boardman St.` is NCDMM's $108,000
exactly. Two buildings are wholly let and the model says so, at an implied
$6.94 and $6.99 a square foot — which is what makes the $7.00 credible
rather than chosen.

**Three things to know about it:**

* **The shares are what the rate uses.** If every building is 10% larger than
  this, the carve-out does not move at all. The *split* is what matters.
* **No vacancy is claimed, and this is the one place the estimate is not on
  the low side.** Nothing on the record measures vacant lettable space, so
  none is recorded — and the audited statements say the property is
  predominately *available* as operating leases, which includes space
  standing empty. Any vacancy Heidi measures makes the carve-out larger and
  the federal rate lower.
* **A known error, in YBI's favour and left uncorrected.** Named tenants
  already reimburse **$100,931.51** into the overhead accounts — Steelite for
  property tax, NCDMM for Boardman electric and gas, and six others. The
  engine carves a share of the pool *net of those credits*, so that money
  leaves twice, by $100,931.51 × (1 − share). It is recorded here rather than
  fixed because which credits are tenant recovery is a judgment, and it errs
  **against** YBI. Settle it before the real square footage arrives, so the
  first measurement produces the right answer.

## 2. The defensible upward movements

Each of these raises the rate, each is supported, and none of them is applied.

| | combined rate | why it is defensible |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **as recorded** | **24.71%** | the low side of both estimates |
| only the three assets a document names | 25.13% | the covered rule is an aggregate argument; an auditor entitled to ask for the invoice would get the three |
| EDA's own 80.09% share on TBB5 Phase-2 | 25.22% | the closeout letter states the share. The other 19.91% is YBI's and its depreciation is allowable |
| let space at $8.00 a square foot | 26.71% | a higher market rent means the same rent roll occupies less space |
| **both together** | **27.12%** | the top of the band the documents support |

And the movements the other way, which are just as real:

| | combined rate |
| --- | ---: |
| 5% of the estate vacant | 23.13% |
| let space at $6.00 a square foot | 22.05% |
| 10% of the estate vacant | 21.55% |
| **$6.00, 10% vacant, covered assets** | **18.89%** |

**The band is 18.89% to 27.12%.** The fringe rate is not in it: both of its
parts are anchored to source documents — the P&L's six fringe accounts over
the payroll register — so 21.90% holds in every scenario that does not move
an account into the fringe pool.

These are arithmetic on two recorded figures, `v_pool_balance.gross` and
`rate.base_amount`, with a different carve-out substituted. Nothing in the
model is re-derived. The engine's own figure for the recorded scenario is
24.71% and the same arithmetic gives 24.72%, the difference being that the
engine rounds each building's share to six places and this rounds the
estate's once.

## 3. What the rate does to the America Makes awards

All four restated on the certified rate and accepted:

| award | invoices | billed | indirect billed | indirect supported | to claim | to return |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Drive AM | 12 | $579,240.87 | $0.00 | $89,314.76 | — | **$128,474.23** |
| Digital Engineering | 7 | $579,074.25 | $0.00 | $51,248.26 | — | **$320,427.12** |
| Last Tactical Mile | 12 | $368,222.24 | $44,400.00 | $81,669.78 | **$43,960.60** | — |
| Hybrid Phase II | 9 | $187,416.05 | $0.00 | $22,680.85 | — | **$72,947.07** |

**The two columns are never added together.** $43,960.60 to ask for and
$521,848.42 to give back are two conversations, and a net figure of
$477,887.82 would settle both in one line and say nothing about either.

Three of the four billed **no indirect line at all**, and that is not a
saving — it is the finding. The recovery was inside a loaded labour rate,
which 200.414(f) does not contemplate: it charges the de minimis as a rate on
MTDC and says nothing about indirect embedded in an undisclosed labour rate.
So restating is a **rebuild**, not an addition — the labour line comes down
to wages plus fringe before the indirect line goes on — and on three of these
awards the rebuild finds that more was collected than the year supports.

**Only Last Tactical Mile budgets indirect** and it is the only one running
the other way. Its Schedule B carries 10.00% of its whole direct cost, to
four decimal places, which is the sole place in any executed document where
the de minimis election is visible at all.

**The amendment memorandum and the acceptance form travel with each one.**
Drive AM's and Digital Engineering's agreements carry **no change-of-basis
clause on the record**, so their memoranda ask NCDMM to name the instrument
rather than citing one — which is a fact about the agreements, not an
omission in the paper.

## 4. What is not finished, and what it would move

* **The citations.** 0 of 363 federally chargeable judgments cite a document.
  No figure here changes when they land; what changes is the evidence grade
  every one of them can reach, and `VERIFIED` counts citations.
* **`evidence_ratio` is 0.0000 on every objective.** The whole labour
  distribution is management reconstruction and no certification has been
  signed. It does not move the rate. It goes to whether 200.430(i) supports
  the direct labour charge at all.
* **The square footage, measured rather than derived.** The single largest
  input, and the whole of the band's width below 24.71%.
* **The double-counted tenant credits**, §1.1 above — $100,931.51, against
  YBI, uncorrected on purpose.
* **ARC PW-20599.** $1,215,338 of federal expenditure across 2023 and 2024
  and only $120,310 of it named against an asset.

None of it blocks anything. Every document in this set carries the band and
repeats the unfinished steps above its own figures, because a paper that is
silent about what it rests on leaves the reader to assume, and the assumption
made about a figure on a letterhead is the generous one.
